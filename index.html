Our Story

A Family Table in the Heart of Pigadia

Tucked between the waves at Afoti–Limniatis — across from Konstantinos Palace and beside Blue Bay Hotel — To Perasma is where Karpathos comes to eat like family. Every plate is built on homemade recipes passed down through generations, fresh island produce, and fish straight from the morning boats.
